I co-lead the Launch Operations sub-team for Project EXO Bronco at Bronco Launch, Cal Poly Pomona's high power rocketry organisation. EXO Bronco is a two-stage rocket aimed at the Karman line. I replaced the corroded deployment terminals in the avionics bay, integrated the sleds, and commanded the two-stage pop test that fired correctly on both stages on 30 August 2026.

Before that I designed and built two scratch high-power rockets in SolidWorks and flew them. I earned my NAR Level 2 certification on the first one at 4,135 ft, after an earlier attempt failed on recovery and had to be rebuilt. The second went supersonic at Mach 1.2 and came apart in flight at 2.20 seconds. The avionics bay came down under its own parachute with the complete flight record on it, which is the only reason I could reconstruct what happened and specify the redesign that followed.

Four documented flight tests across two vehicles. Two of them failed. The portfolio covers all four.
